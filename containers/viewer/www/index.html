VICE starts on the live screen, at real speed and with audio. In the big screen the PC keyboard reaches the C64; the buttons at the top turn on Keyboard (on-screen C64 keyboard), Joy 1, Joy 2 and Audio. From the VICE menu (File → Smart attach) any file of the project can be attached, in /work. Your own files (.d64 .g64 .prg .t64 .crt) copied into out/tmp/ show up above. From the shell:

lab64 play x64sc <options>
