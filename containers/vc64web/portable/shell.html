3 · Joystick and keyboard
Only for a real keyboard: with the option on, SPACE becomes Fire and cannot be typed. Effective from the next start.
Test
Restarts the C64 and draws the keyboard: the pressed key turns reversed, the keys already tested turn green. It includes left/right SHIFT, F1–F8 (F2/F4/F6/F8 = SHIFT+F1/F3/F5/F7), RESTORE and Stick/Fire of ports 1 and 2. It reacts within 1 frame (20 ms). To exit use Reset.
